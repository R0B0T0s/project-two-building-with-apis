About Movie Rental

Movie data from TMDB

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Movie titles, overviews, posters and genres come from The Movie Database (TMDB). Renting here is simulated: no payments are taken and no movies are streamed.

Who can see your data

Employees and admins can see all data, including Private movies and notes, so that they can support users.

Every movie you save is Private by default. You can make it Public, so anyone can see it, or Shared with specific usernames. Usernames are shown publicly.
